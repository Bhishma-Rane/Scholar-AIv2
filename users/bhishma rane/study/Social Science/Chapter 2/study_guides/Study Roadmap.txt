Here is a step-by-step numbered learning roadmap for Chapter 2: Reshaping India's Political Map:

**Phase 1: Understanding the Context**

1. Read and understand the introduction to the chapter, including the concept of the "medieval period" in Indian history.
2. Familiarize yourself with the timeline provided (Fig. 2.1) and note important dates such as the establishment of the Delhi Sultanate (1206), the Vijayanagara Empire (1336), and the arrival of the Portuguese in India (1498).

**Phase 2: The Rise and Fall of the Delhi Sultanate**

3. Study the rise and fall of the Delhi Sultanate, including the rule of five successive foreign dynasties of Turkic-Afghan origin.
4. Analyze the impact of the Sultanate period on Indian society, including military campaigns, territorial expansion, and the plundering of temples and seats of learning.

**Phase 3: The Reigns of Ala-ud-din Khilji and Muhammad bin Tughlaq**

5. Learn about the reigns of Ala-ud-din Khilji (1296-1316) and Muhammad bin Tughlaq (1320-1351), including their military campaigns, territorial expansions, and economic policies.
6. Discuss the significance of these rulers in shaping Indian history, including their impact on art, culture, and administration.

**Phase 4: The Economy and Society**

7. Explore the economy during this period, including the use of token currency, taxes, and slave trade.
8. Analyze the social implications of the Sultanate period, including the lives of common people, the role of women, and the impact on art and culture.

**Phase 5: Review and Reflection**

9. Review the key events and figures discussed in the chapter.
10. Reflect on how the Sultanate period shaped Indian history and society, and consider its relevance to contemporary India.

By following this roadmap, you should gain a deeper understanding of the complex political landscape of medieval India and the significant events that shaped the country's history.
